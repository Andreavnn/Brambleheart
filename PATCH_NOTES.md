- Rebuilds Battle presentation around Combat Encounters, Damage, Health Points, Healing, Fate Rolls, Resistance/Weakness, and current end-of-round guidance.

## Current game changes

- Arcane Command may be used a second time once per turn without another Core Combat opportunity. The additional cast must be an Enhance or Hex Spell and costs [+1] Mana.
- Projectile weapons apply condition [-2] to Strike when attacking within [1] square. Skyfire deals an additional [2] Lethal damage, and Slashing deals [1] Lethal damage when its trigger is met.
- Health states use the current thresholds: Wounded at [8] or less Health, Critical at [4] or less, Last Breath at [2] or less, and Defeated at [0]. Only the most severe applicable state applies.
- Defeated characters make a Fate Roll on their next turn in the following round: [6+] passes, [5] or less adds a Fate Mark, and restoring at least [1] Health removes all Fate Marks.
- Threat Level now provides a current five-Attribute-compatible character/group framework. Individual Threat is based on advancement beyond the baseline hero; Group Threat is the sum of the party's individual Threat Levels. Monster and encounter calibration remains reserved for the later Watcher rules pass.
