**Site Update — Beta 0.25**  
**Game Update — v0.13**

## Current game changes

- Standard-damage Melee and Ranged weapons gain [+1] base damage. Penetration weapons retain their existing Direct-damage values.
- Deadly weapons list damage as `X [Y]`: `X` is normal weapon damage and `[Y]` is fixed Lethal damage dealt when the Strike succeeds.
- Dual Wielding applies [-2] to Strike rolls made with the wielded weapons. Shared qualities apply only once; Versatile provides one [+1], reducing the normal penalty to [-1]. Beastgrasp increases the base Dual Wielding Strike penalty to [-4] while wielding two two-handed weapons.
- Ward Guard is retired. Turnstrike grants Ward equal to half the weapon's normal, non-bracketed damage value, rounded up, with a minimum of [+1].
- Standard, Direct, and Lethal remain the Damage Categories. On-Going is a recurring-damage qualifier that keeps its stated category and prevents normal damage additions from being applied again to later ticks.
- Typical advancement pacing is 7–10 XP per session. Attribute, Skill, new Skill, Talent, and Magic Level costs are modestly increased so one or two sessions usually support one or two upgrades depending on how XP is spent.
- A Spark-aligned kept Deed grants [+1] bonus XP, limited to once per session, so Spark rewards reinforce play without overwhelming the normal advancement pace.
- Tordan Steady Pace uses its current Move and minimum-Speed rules. Cethra Poised Reflex and Rivkan Double Lilly are limited to once per round.
- Auravex Cycle Of The Beast restores only [1] Health or [1] Mana to the host when its host rider triggers.
- Braelor Thunderstep increases Hero's Charge movement by [+2] squares and its Power Through deals [2] Lethal damage before the existing forced-movement rider.
- Urnath Frostwalker grants Resistance [+2] to Cold and Natural Terrain grants [+2] Ward.
- The obsolete six-Attribute Encounter Rating calculation is retired. Threat Level remains the current Watcher-facing comparative framework until that section receives its dedicated rules pass.
